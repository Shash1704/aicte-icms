# SIH 2026 — Problem Statement SIH26264

**Title:** Integrated Dashboard for Monitoring and Tracking Institution-Related Complaint Cases Across AICTE Bureaus/Cells
**Organization:** AICTE · **Department:** Regulation Bureau · **Category:** Software · **Theme:** Smart Automation
**Source:** https://sih.gov.in/sih2026PS (no dataset provided)

## Problem
AICTE receives complaints and grievances about technical institutions from students, faculty and other stakeholders. They are handled by different Bureaus/Cells, including the Regulation Bureau (RB) and the Public Grievance Redressal Cell (PGRC). Cases about the same institution may be received and processed by different Bureaus/Cells. With no centralized, real-time monitoring, this leads to overlapping actions, duplicated effort, delays in disposal, and difficulty knowing which Bureau/Cell is handling a given institution/case.

## Objective — a single-window dashboard where authorized officials can
- View all institution-related complaint/grievance cases in one place.
- Identify the Bureau/Cell currently handling each case.
- Track the complete status and movement of a complaint/case.
- Identify cases where RB and PGRC actions overlap, or multiple Bureaus/Cells deal with the same institution.
- Avoid duplication of action and ensure coordination.
- Monitor pending, disposed, under-process, transferred and escalated cases.
- Generate institution-, region-, Bureau/Cell- and status-wise reports.
- Get alerts for pending cases and cases approaching prescribed timelines.

## Key features expected
1. **Centralized Institution Profile** — AICTE Permanent ID; name, address, region, other details; consolidated complaint history.
2. **Complaint/Case Tracking** — unique Case ID; date of receipt; nature/category; current status; assigned Bureau/Cell; assigned officer; last action taken; next action required; date of last action; expected disposal date.
3. **Cross-Bureau/Cell Overlap Detection** — automatically identify when complaints on the same institution are handled by RB, PGRC or others; overlap alert; link/map related complaints and cases.
4. **Real-Time Dashboard** — total received; pending; under process; disposed; transferred; pending beyond prescribed timelines; institution-wise status; Bureau/Cell-wise workload.
5. **Workflow and Action History** — chronological history; officer/Bureau responsible; timestamps; remarks and supporting documents; transfer/escalation history.
6. **Search and Filters** — institution name; AICTE Permanent ID; Case ID; Bureau/Cell; region; category; status; date range.
7. **Alerts and Notifications** — duplicate/overlap alerts; pending reminders; escalation alerts; timeline/SLA alerts.
8. **Reports and Analytics** — Bureau-wise; region-wise; institution-wise history; pending-case ageing; disposal rate; overlapping/duplicate cases; monthly/quarterly performance.

## Innovation expected
Use data matching, rule-based logic and/or AI-assisted analytics to identify complaints relating to the same institution even when descriptions or case references differ. E.g. Institution A → PGRC → under process; Institution A → RB → action initiated ⇒ show **"Potential Overlapping Cases – Coordination Required"**.

## Expected outcome
A single source of truth for institution-related complaints and regulatory actions: less duplication, no conflicting/parallel actions, better RB/PGRC/other coordination, timely disposal, accountability and transparency, real-time monitoring, data-driven decisions.

## Target users
RB, PGRC, other AICTE Bureaus/Cells, Regional Offices/Centres, authorized officers and senior management, Competent Authority/AICTE administration.
